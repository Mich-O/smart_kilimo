Irrigation advisory engine

Tell farmers exactly when their crops need water — before they lose the season to guesswork.

Smart Kilimo runs a FAO-56 water balance calculation against real rainfall data for every registered plot, every day. When a plot's soil moisture deficit crosses its crop's threshold, the farmer gets a message telling them how much to water — nothing more, nothing less. No smartphone required: the same engine runs behind USSD and SMS as it does here on the web.
